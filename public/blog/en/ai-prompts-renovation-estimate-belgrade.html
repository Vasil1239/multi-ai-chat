5 AI prompts for a Belgrade renovation estimate

Ready templates for base work, cosmetic renovation and materials. With real 2026 euro pricing.

Updated: September 11, 2026

How to use

  1. Open AskHub and switch to Paid mode.
  2. Pick Claude Sonnet 4.5 or GPT‑5 — they estimate best.
  3. Copy a prompt, plug in area, district and apartment condition.

Prompt 1. Full base finishing

Estimate a full base finishing of a {AREA} sqm apartment in Belgrade, {DISTRICT} district. Include: demolition, screed, plaster, electrical and plumbing rough-in, sound insulation, windows, entrance door. Provide 2026 euro prices per item and a total. Mid-market Belgrade level, no premium brands.

Prompt 2. Cosmetic renovation

Estimate a cosmetic renovation of a {AREA} sqm apartment in Belgrade: paint walls and ceilings, replace flooring (mid-range laminate), replace bathroom fixtures, replace interior doors. Break down materials + labor per room. 2026 euros.

Prompt 3. Bathroom turnkey

Detailed estimate for a 4 sqm bathroom in Belgrade: waterproofing, tiling, bathtub/shower, toilet, sink, towel rail, ventilation. Two options — budget (~€1500–2500) and mid (~€3500–5000). Materials and labor separately.

Prompt 4. Kitchen

Cost of a kitchen in Belgrade: {LENGTH} m body, countertop, built-in appliances (hob, oven, hood, dishwasher), sink with faucet, install. Mid-market segment, 2026. Total in euros.

Prompt 5. Contractor quote review

Review the contractor quote (below) for overpriced items and missing lines. Compare with 2026 Belgrade market. Point out where to save and what the contractor may have forgotten.\n\nQUOTE:\n{QUOTE_TEXT}
