What is a token

A token is a chunk of text, typically 3–4 characters. On average 1000 tokens ≈ 750 English words. Cyrillic and Cyrillic-script languages consume 40–60% more tokens per word.

Providers bill separately for input tokens (what you send) and output tokens (what the model writes). Output is usually 3–5× more expensive.

Real-world example

You feed the model a 2000-word article (~2700 tokens) and ask for a 300-word summary (~400 output):

  • GPT‑5: $0.0034 + $0.004 ≈ $0.007
  • Claude Sonnet 4.5: $0.008 + $0.006 ≈ $0.014
  • Gemini 2.5 Flash: $0.0008 + $0.001 ≈ $0.002

One summary costs less than a US cent even on the flagship. A thousand summaries — $2 to $14.

How to save

  1. Short system prompts. Don't say "you are a world-class assistant…" — say "be concise".
  2. Simple tasks → Flash / Haiku / DeepSeek. Complex → Sonnet / GPT‑5.
  3. Don't upload the whole document — clip the relevant section.
  4. Prompt caching (Claude and Gemini) gives up to 90% off repeated system prompts.
